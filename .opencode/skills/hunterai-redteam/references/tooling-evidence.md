# Tooling, research, evidence, and validation

Select scanners, fuzzers, reverse-engineering tools, and exploit-analysis tooling from the reachable technology and requested outcome. Record versions and configuration. Preserve raw output and validate material results against source, runtime behavior, or independent reproduction.

Build automation with explicit inputs, destinations, concurrency, timeouts, retries, exclusions, resume, and structured exports. Do not silently enable telemetry, credentials, hooks, persistent services, or unrelated destinations.

For CVEs, establish affected versions, patch commits, root cause, reachability, prerequisites, proof quality, exploitability, mitigations, and regression conditions. For variant analysis, generalize source, transformation, sink, and missing control, then encode positive and negative fixtures.

Validate each finding by identifying the component and revision, attacker-controlled input, missing or bypassed control, observable effect, independent reproduction, strongest benign refutation, and acceptance test. Keep leads, confirmed findings, and unverified claims separate.

Store exact commands, requests, responses, traces, hashes, timestamps, test identities, and before/after state. Preserve raw operational evidence separately from display-masked reports. Reports state Executed, Observed, Inferred, or Unverified and do not inflate impact beyond the demonstrated boundary.
