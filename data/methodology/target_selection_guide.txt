-Bug Bounty Program (BBP): is when a company offers monetary rewards for valid security vulnerabilities.

You Get:

1-Money
2-Hall of Fame
3-Reputation
4-Sometimes swag

-Vulnerability Disclosure Program (VDP): allows you to report vulnerabilities, but No guaranteed payment

You Get:

1-Hall of Fame
2-Sometimes swag
3-Sometimes discretionary rewards
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

-Platforms Programs: hosted on platforms like:

1-HackerOne
2-Bugcrowd
3-Intigriti

-External  Programs/Self-Hosted Programs: These are programs NOT on HackerOne/Bugcrowd.

They run on:

1-Company website --> https://t.me/ForBugHunters/13
2-security@company.com --> https://mailmeteor.com/email-checker
